About me

I'm Suroor Khan, a graphic designer, video editor and IT specialist based in Nowshera, Pakistan. I create posters, brand visuals, video edits, app interfaces and websites that are clear, polished and built to get results.

My portfolio covers product promotions, fashion and beauty, food, marketing campaigns and a complete visual set for uniCare, a hospital app, from banners and icons to product images.

I am also an AI practitioner. I design and build with AI tools, write the prompts that direct them and automate repetitive work, so projects move faster while the creative direction and the quality stay in my hands.

As a trainer, I teach students design, editing and technical skills, and I share the templates and guides I use myself.
